Australian Clinical Reference & Practice Quick-Guide

Standard normal reference intervals, MET call escalation limits, and HITH high-risk medication guidelines.

Pathology Normal Ranges (AU)

  • Fasting Blood Glucose: 4.0 - 7.0 mmol/L
  • Random Glucose: 4.0 - 11.0 mmol/L
  • Potassium (K+): 3.5 - 5.2 mmol/L
  • Sodium (Na+): 135 - 145 mmol/L
  • eGFR (Renal): > 90 mL/min/1.73m²
  • Creatinine: 60 - 110 µmol/L
  • Magnesium (Mg2+): 0.7 - 1.0 mmol/L
  • Hb (Female/Male): 115-165 / 130-185 g/L

MET / HITH Escalation Limits

  • Respiratory Rate: < 8 or > 25 bpm
  • SpO2 (Room Air): < 92% (<88% COPD)
  • Systolic BP: < 90 mmHg or > 180 mmHg
  • Heart Rate: < 40 or > 120 bpm
  • Temperature: < 35.5°C or > 38.5°C
  • GCS / Consciousness: Drop > 2 points / New Confusion
  • Urine Output: < 0.5 mL/kg/hr for 2 consecutive hrs

APINCH & HITH High Risk Care

A - Anti-infectives: Check therapeutic drug monitoring (Vancomycin trough, Gentamicin peak/trough) before HITH dose.

P - Potassium / Electrolytes: Replace K+ and Mg2+ pre-methylprednisolone infusion.

I - Insulin: Pre-meal BGLs for all steroid-treated patients; sliding scale active.

N - Narcotics/Sedatives: Opioid risk assessment prior to discharge to home care.

C - Chemotherapy / Steroids: High-dose IV Steroid blood tracking protocol mandatory.

H - Heparin / Anticoagulants: Monitoring anti-Xa / INR in outpatient Deep Vein Thrombosis management.
